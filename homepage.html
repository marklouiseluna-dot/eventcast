About EventCast

EventCast is a lightweight event registration platform built for small organizers — schools, clubs, community centers, and independent planners who need a simple way to receive registrations without paying for expensive software.

Every registration automatically generates a personalized confirmation document that includes a weather forecast and a preparation advisory, so attendees arrive ready for whatever the sky brings.
